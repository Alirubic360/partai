Photograph a used appliance part. PartAi reads the label, drafts an eBay title and description, and shows a price range from similar listings. You check the part number, then export.

Get early access

Built for appliance and HVAC repair shops that resell used parts. Free tier planned. No spam.

How it works
  1. Take a photo of the part or its label (add a 10-second voice note if you like).
  2. PartAi reads the brand, part number and model, and drafts the listing.
  3. You confirm the part number, then download a CSV for eBay or your inventory sheet.
What it does not do

It does not guarantee a part fits or that it works. It never guesses a blurry part number: it flags it for you to check. Prices are estimates from comparable listings, not promises of what a part will sell for. The product is in testing, so features may change.
